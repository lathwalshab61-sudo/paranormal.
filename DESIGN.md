## Brand & Style

This design system blends tactical occult research with high-precision surveillance telemetry. The brand evokes calculated tension, rigorous paranormal classification, and investigative immersion. It treats the unexplained not with camp, but with cold, classified institutional authority.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactical Cyber-Minimalism** with **Dark Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-deep obsidian voids anchored by subtle radar grid overlays and scanning line artifacts.
- Spectral purple and warning-state crimson signaling anomalous activity, spectral density, and hazardous containment thresholds.
- Cyan telemetry accents delivering clinical clarity for sensors, coordinates, and EMF telemetry metrics.
- Surfaces are frosted, semi-transparent layers suspended above dark field gradients, edged with hairline luminous borders and ghostly back-refractions.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for high-contrast, low-light operations. Surfaces progress through three tiers of deep obsidian and cosmic soot:
- **Base Surface (`#0B0B0F`)**: Canvas backdrop representing the field void.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#12111A`)**: Structural panels, tactical sidebars, and viewport containers.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#181622`)**: Foreground cards, floating toolbars, and active tactical windows.

**Color Roles**:
- **Spectral Violet (`#8B5CF6` / `#6B21A8`)**: Represents supernatural entities, aura frequencies, phase-shifts, and brand focal anchors.
- **Telemetry Cyan (`#06B6D4` / `#22D3EE` / `#38BDF8`)**: Drives technical readouts, coordinates, active trackers, telemetry data stream toggles, and safe-status indicators.
- **Hazard Blood-Red (`#EF4444` / `#DC2626`)**: Critical breach warnings, EMF level-5 anomalies, audio spike alerts, and immediate containment overrides.
- **Border Gradients**: Fine lines utilize `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)` blending into `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)` across deep dark borders.

## Typography

Typography establishes an investigative hierarchy balancing scientific inquiry with field tactical documentation:
- **Headlines & Body**: Rendered in **Space Grotesk**. Its sharp mechanical proportions and geometric terminals project a clinical, forward-operating attitude while retaining legibility across dense log screens.
- **Telemetry, Timestamps, & Data Points**: Exclusively set in **Space Mono**. All sensor outputs, GPS coordinates, EVP frequencies, and case file index numbers are rendered in monospace uppercase with expanded letter-spacing to reinforce institutional classification.
- Headings maintain tightened negative tracking, while all telemetry labels feature wide kerning (`0.08em`–`0.15em`) for military-grade data scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column tactical dashboard fluid grid designed for multi-sensor display arrays:
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Layout accommodates fixed lateral sensor telemetry alongside primary mission consoles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Secondary readouts collapse into modal drawers or collapsible lower decks.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Critical warning states and field logging occupy full-width single-column blocks.

Components observe a baseline 4px/8px rhythm. Panel interiors maintain disciplined padding: `space-md` (`1rem`) for dense metric cards and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) for primary evidence dossier frames.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined through physical luminescence and translucent refraction rather than traditional drop shadows:
- **Level 0 (Canvas Void)**: Solid `#0B0B0F` underlaid with a subtle `#181622` grid line pattern (24px squares, 1px lines at 15% opacity).
- **Level 1 (Sub-Panels & Nav)**: `background: rgba(18, 17, 26, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, edged by a 1px border of `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Modules)**: `background: rgba(24, 22, 34, 0.65)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, framed with a 1px border gradient transitioning from `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)` to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`.
- **Luminescent Overlays & Ghost Glows**:
  - *Spectral Elevation*: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25), inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
  - *Sensor Focus*: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`
  - *Hazard Breach*: `box-shadow: 0 0 30px -2px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.45), inset 0 0 12px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.2)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces a semi-chiseled, tactical silhouette with subtle softened corners (`roundedness: 1`):
- Standard cards, interactive controls, and modal dialogs use `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- Larger surveillance enclosures and video playback bounds use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Status beacons, radar pings, and notification tags may feature sharp diagonal chamfers (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to emphasize a military reconnaissance HUD feel.
- Buttons and chips never use round pill geometry; corners remain clipped and structured.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Spectral Action)**: `#8B5CF6` solid fill or heavy semi-transparent purple gradient with white text (`Space Grotesk`, medium), 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, and a subtle ambient purple glow (`0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Telemetry Action)**: `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)` fill, hairline border in `#06B6D4`, text in `#22D3EE` (`Space Mono`). Hover state triggers border luminance and `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)` fill.
- **Destructive / Containment**: Solid `#DC2626` or high-intensity outline `#EF4444` with pulsating crimson drop-glow.

### Chips & Badges
- Monospaced indicator tags (`Space Mono`, 10px uppercase).
- Surrounded by hairline borders with mini status pings (e.g., solid 6px pulsating dot).
- Variants: `Class IV Entity` (Purple glow), `Telemetry Active` (Cyan pulse), `EMF Level 5` (Blood-red alert).

### Cards & Dossiers
- Framed in frosted obsidian glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`).
- Equipped with tactical corner crosshairs (`+`) or sub-pixel coordinate tags in the card header.
- Hairline top border with dual-tone gradient (`#8B5CF6` fading into `#06B6D4`).

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Dark inset backgrounds (`#0B0B0F` at 80% opacity) with `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.
- Input text rendered in `#F8FAFC`.
- Focus state drops cyan anchor line glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`) and sharpens the border to `#06B6D4`.
- Accompanying field label in `Space Mono` uppercase.

### Radar & Telemetry Readouts
- Concentric circular and crosshair vector overlays tinted in `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`.
- Live telemetry streams displayed in tabular numerical columns using `Space Mono`.
- Real-time anomalies marked by soft radial blur blips in `#EF4444` or `#8B5CF6`.

### Checkboxes & Switches
- Checkboxes: 16x16px square with 2px radius, filled with obsidian, outlined in cyan. Checked state presents a solid cyan inner square with edge bloom.
- Toggles: Rectangular sliding track with sharp 2px corners, moving a glowing square indicator knob between inactive `#181622` and active `#06B6D4`.
